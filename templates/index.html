Frequently asked questions

Questions about ITSupport?

Here are a few things to know before submitting a request.

What is ITSupport?
ITSupport is a helpdesk application for reporting technical issues and tracking support requests from submission to resolution.
How do I report an IT issue?
Create an account or log in, open the ticket creation page, fill in the required details, and submit your request.
What is a ticket code?
A ticket code is a reference assigned to your support request. You can use it to find the corresponding ticket in your account.
Can I check the status of my ticket?
Yes. Open My Tickets to see your requests, their current statuses, and the details recorded for each ticket.
Can I attach a screenshot to my ticket?
If file attachments are enabled on the ticket form, you can attach a relevant screenshot or supporting file to help explain the issue.
What should I do if my issue is still unresolved?
Check your ticket for the latest status and available updates. If you still need assistance, contact your IT support team through the available support channel.
